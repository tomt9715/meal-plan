Simple Lentil Soup with Roasted Vegetables

Hearty, warming and incredibly easy.

Prep
10 min
Cook
25 min
Serves
3
Total
10 min + 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 4 cups low sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, squeeze of lemon

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot. Add onion, carrot, celery — cook 5 min.
  2. Add garlic, cumin and turmeric — cook 1 min until fragrant.
  3. Add rinsed lentils and broth. Bring to boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer 20–22 min until lentils are tender.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon before serving.
  6. Optional: blend half the soup for a creamier texture.

💚 Why it's good for you

Lentils are one of the most powerful foods for lowering LDL cholesterol and stabilizing blood sugar. One cup of cooked lentils provides 16g of fiber — more than half the recommended daily intake.
