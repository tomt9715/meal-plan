Greek Chicken Bowl

Fresh, filling and comes together fast.

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa or brown rice
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ red onion, sliced
  • ½ cup kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt as tzatziki base + fresh dill

Instructions

  1. Season chicken with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook in a pan over medium-high heat, 5–6 min per side. Rest 3 min, then slice.
  3. Assemble bowls: quinoa or rice on the bottom.
  4. Top with sliced chicken, cucumber, tomatoes, red onion and olives.
  5. Drizzle with a little extra olive oil and lemon juice.
  6. Add a dollop of Greek yogurt with dill on the side if using.

💚 Why it's good for you

This bowl delivers complete protein, fiber and heart-healthy fats in one dish. The Mediterranean combination of olive oil, lemon and oregano provides anti-inflammatory polyphenols that protect LDL from oxidation.
