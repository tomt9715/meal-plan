Turkey & Veggie Whole Wheat Pasta

Comfort food that's actually good for you.

Prep
8 min
Cook
20 min
Serves
3

Ingredients

  • 8 oz whole wheat penne or spaghetti
  • ½ lb ground turkey (93% lean)
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes (no salt added)
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 cup spinach
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt, pepper, red chili flakes
  • Fresh basil to garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta per package instructions. Reserve ¼ cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan. Add onion — cook 4 min until softened.
  3. Add garlic — cook 1 min. Add ground turkey, break apart, cook 5 min until browned.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper and chili flakes.
  5. Simmer sauce 8 min. Add zucchini last 3 min, spinach last 1 min.
  6. Toss drained pasta with sauce. Add a splash of pasta water if needed.
  7. Serve topped with fresh basil.

💚 Why it's good for you

Whole wheat pasta has triple the fiber of regular pasta and a significantly lower glycemic index. Ground turkey is lean protein that supports muscle maintenance while keeping saturated fat low.
