Walnuts & Grapes

A snack with surprising heart benefits.

Prep
1 min
Cook
0 min
Serves
1
Total
1 min + 0 min

Ingredients

  • 20–25 walnuts (about 1 oz)
  • ½ cup grapes

Instructions

  1. Rinse grapes.
  2. Combine in a bowl.
  3. Portion walnuts — about a small handful.

💚 Why it's good for you

Walnuts are the only tree nut significantly rich in ALA omega-3 fatty acids, and they're proven to reduce total and LDL cholesterol. Grapes contain resveratrol, a polyphenol associated with cardiovascular protection.
