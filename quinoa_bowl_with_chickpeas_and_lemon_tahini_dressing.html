Quinoa Bowl with Chickpeas & Lemon-Tahini Dressing

A filling bowl with complete protein and incredible flavor.

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
2
Total
10 min + 15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup quinoa
  • 2 cups low sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • For dressing: 2 tbsp tahini, juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove, 2 tbsp water, salt

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa in broth: bring to boil, reduce and simmer 13–15 min covered.
  2. Roast zucchini and bell pepper with 1 tbsp olive oil at 400°F for 15 min.
  3. Make dressing: whisk tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic and water until smooth.
  4. Warm chickpeas in remaining olive oil in a pan, 3 min.
  5. Fluff quinoa. Assemble bowls with quinoa, roasted veg and chickpeas.
  6. Drizzle generously with tahini dressing.

💚 Why it's good for you

Tahini (sesame paste) is rich in sesamin, a lignan that reduces LDL cholesterol. Quinoa provides all 9 essential amino acids and has a lower glycemic impact than most grains.
