Almonds & Apple

The perfect blood sugar-stabilizing snack.

Prep
1 min
Cook
0 min
Serves
1
Total
1 min + 0 min

Ingredients

  • 1 medium apple
  • 20–25 almonds (about 1 small handful)

Instructions

  1. Wash and slice apple (or eat whole).
  2. Portion almonds into a small bowl or bag.
  3. Eat almonds first, then apple — the fat from nuts slows sugar absorption.

💚 Why it's good for you

Almonds are one of the most heart-healthy snacks available — they lower LDL cholesterol and reduce post-meal blood sugar spikes. The fiber in the apple combined with almond fat creates a snack with minimal glycemic impact.
