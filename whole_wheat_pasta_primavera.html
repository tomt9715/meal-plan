Whole Wheat Pasta Primavera

Light, fresh and loaded with vegetables.

Prep
8 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 6 oz whole wheat penne
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 1 cup spinach
  • ½ cup peas (frozen, thawed)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt, pepper, red chili flakes
  • Fresh basil and Parmesan (optional, light)

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta per package instructions. Reserve ¼ cup pasta water.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Add garlic — cook 1 min. Add zucchini — cook 3 min.
  4. Add cherry tomatoes — cook 2 min until they start to burst.
  5. Add peas and spinach — stir 1 min until spinach wilts.
  6. Drain pasta and toss with vegetables. Add pasta water as needed.
  7. Squeeze lemon over everything, season with salt, pepper and chili flakes.
  8. Top with fresh basil and a light sprinkle of Parmesan if using.

💚 Why it's good for you

Whole wheat pasta has a glycemic index nearly 40% lower than regular pasta, making it significantly better for blood sugar management. The abundance of vegetables provides fiber, antioxidants and micronutrients.
