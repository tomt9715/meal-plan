Chicken & Vegetable Stir Fry over Cauliflower Rice

All the satisfaction of a stir fry, without the white rice.

Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Serves
2
Total
10 min + 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts, thinly sliced
  • 1 head cauliflower, riced (or 1 bag frozen cauliflower rice)
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp low sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • Squeeze of lime

Instructions

  1. Rice cauliflower: pulse in food processor until rice-sized, or use frozen. Set aside.
  2. Season chicken with a pinch of salt. Stir fry in 1 tbsp olive oil on high heat 4–5 min.
  3. Remove chicken. In same pan, add remaining olive oil. Stir fry onion and garlic 2 min.
  4. Add broccoli and zucchini, stir fry 3–4 min until tender-crisp.
  5. Add chicken back. Add soy sauce, ginger and sesame oil. Toss everything 1 min.
  6. In separate pan, sauté cauliflower rice in a little olive oil 3–4 min. Season with salt.
  7. Serve stir fry over cauliflower rice with a squeeze of lime.

💚 Why it's good for you

Cauliflower rice dramatically reduces the carbohydrate load compared to white rice, making this dish much more blood-sugar friendly. The variety of vegetables provides diverse antioxidants and fiber.
