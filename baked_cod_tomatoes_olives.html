Baked Cod with Tomatoes, Olives & Quinoa

Mediterranean flavors in one easy baking dish.

Prep
5 min
Cook
20 min
Serves
2

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets
  • 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
  • ½ cup kalamata olives, halved
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp red chili flakes
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh parsley
  • Quinoa to serve

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Mix tomatoes, olives, garlic, olive oil, oregano and chili flakes in a baking dish.
  3. Nestle cod fillets into the tomato mixture. Season fish with salt and pepper.
  4. Spoon some of the tomato mixture over the fish.
  5. Bake 18–20 min until cod flakes easily with a fork.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley. Serve over quinoa.

💚 Why it's good for you

This one-dish Mediterranean meal combines omega-3s from cod with the heart-protective oleic acid in olives and olive oil. Tomatoes provide lycopene, a carotenoid that reduces LDL oxidation.
